About Nenne

Nenne is a Japanese baby word for sleeping. This app supports parents of babies aged 0 to 24 months.

It helps you track active time (wake time and naps), nursing and formula timing, and caffeine while nursing. Enter the wake time, and the background changes from soft blue to orange and then purple as the recommended nap time gets closer.

Nenne gives general guidance based on typical ranges. Every baby is different. It does not replace advice from your doctor or nurse.
